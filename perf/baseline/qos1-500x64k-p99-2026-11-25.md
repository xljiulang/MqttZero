# QoS 1 500x64 KiB p99 Optimization

## Target

- MQTT 5
- QoS 1
- 500 subscribers
- 64 KiB payload
- Three repetitions, 5-second warmup, 20-second measurement
- MQTTnet Client used for both Broker comparisons

## Baseline

| Stage | MQTTZero throughput | MQTTnet throughput | Ratio | MQTTZero p99 | MQTTnet p99 | P99 ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Before optimization | 4.049 msg/s | 4.000 msg/s | 1.012x | 447.8 ms | 325.6 ms | 1.376x |

Diagnostics showed:

- ThreadPool queue was effectively empty.
- GC pauses were not the dominant direct cause at baseline.
- Monitor contention peaked near 498/s versus 17/s for MQTTnet.
- Allocation sampling identified async state-machine boxing in the outbound
  drain and publish send paths.

## Results By Stage

| Stage | MQTTZero throughput | MQTTnet throughput | Ratio | MQTTZero p99 | MQTTnet p99 | P99 ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Task 1: shorter session locks | 14.749 msg/s | 14.104 msg/s | 1.046x | 146.0 ms | 107.4 ms | 1.359x |
| Task 2: allocation-free ACK view | 15.490 msg/s | 14.114 msg/s | 1.098x | 149.6 ms | 96.3 ms | 1.554x |
| Task 3: connection outbound writer | 15.588 msg/s | 13.981 msg/s | 1.115x | 141.8 ms | 94.9 ms | 1.495x |

Task 3 reduced p99 by approximately 5.2% versus Task 2 and preserved the
throughput improvement.

## Diagnostics After Task 3

- Monitor contention peak fell from approximately 498/s to 275/s.
- ThreadPool queue remained near zero.
- Kestrel/Pipe allocation still dominates allocation samples.
- GC pause samples still include occasional long pauses.
- The strict gate remains red because p99 is still above MQTTnet.

## Rejected Experiments

- Raising minimum ThreadPool worker count to 500 made throughput and p99 worse.
- Writing 64 KiB payloads as 16 KiB Pipe chunks made p99 worse.
- Inlining `SendPublishBatchAsync` into the connection writer loop made p99
  worse.

## Conclusion

The branch is not ready to merge or release. Remaining work should continue from
Task 3 using Packet Capture/Kestrel allocation and GC pause attribution, rather
than further scheduling guesses.
